# IR-1 / IR-2 Boundary

This repository currently implements only the clean-room platform skeleton and
IR-2 Foundation Registry V1.

The eight Skill directories, Hermes orchestration directory, and Codex
maintenance directory are namespace placeholders. They contain no business
implementation, formal `SKILL.md`, business CLI, Provider adapter, Product
Library writer, legacy-source dependency, or real network integration.

`viral_research_asset_collection` is an additive Phase 3A namespace placeholder
only. It does not implement Viral Research, create the registered-only Research
Library, call Apify, access the network, download media, or read Legacy Source
Libraries. `ViralResearchRequest`, `ViralResearchPack`, and
`ReferenceCollectionManifest` remain RG-03B candidates and are not registered
Foundation payload identities.

Future cross-Skill business artifacts require RG-03B approval before their
identities and schemas may be added.
